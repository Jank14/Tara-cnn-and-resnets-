Forward (teal): plug numbers in left to right, computing each node — this is model(x).
Backward (coral): once we know the loss, walk back RIGHT TO LEFT, multiplying by the local derivative at each step — this is exactly the chain rule, and it's all loss.backward() is doing. Every backward number = previous backward number × one local slope — nothing more exotic than repeated multiplication.
