Before super().__init__() runs, self._parameters and self._modules don't exist at all — that's WHY it must be the first line. The moment it runs, both dicts are created, empty. Every self.x = ... assignment of a Parameter or a Module after that gets silently filed into one of them by nn.Module.__setattr__ — this is the entire mechanism behind optimizer = Adam(model.parameters()) finding every learnable number, no matter how deeply nested. The numbers shown for each weight are real samples from PyTorch's default nn.Linear init: uniform in [-1/√in_features, 1/√in_features].
Now: what happens when you actually call model(x)?
model(x) routes through nn.Module.__call__, which calls forward() — data flows through exactly the modules that just got registered above, in the order forward() uses them, and the shape changes with every registered module.
