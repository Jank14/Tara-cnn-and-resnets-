The 56-layer PLAIN network is WORSE than the 20-layer one — despite being strictly more expressive on paper.
Add skip connections to the same 56 layers, change nothing else → it becomes the best of all three. Depth is an advantage again.
This replicates He et al.'s 2015 result (training error, not test — ruling out overfitting). The deeper plain network can't find "do nothing extra" as a solution unaided. Skip connections make that the default starting point, so gradient descent just needs to find small improvements on top.
